Privacy

Layer Up NYC · last updated 23 November 2026 · v0.3.0

What Layer Up stores

Your settings (tolerances, units, places) and your wardrobe are stored in your browser. If you sign in with Google, your wardrobe, including a small thumbnail of each garment you photograph, is also stored in your account so it follows you between devices.

Google sign-in

Sign-in is optional. Layer Up asks Google only for your name and email address. It uses them to identify your account and to let Varun Kulkarni, who runs the app, approve your request for server access.

Garment photos

When you add a garment by photo, the photo is resized on your device and sent to an AI model to be read: Google Gemini first, or Groq if Gemini is unavailable. With server access the photo passes through the Layer Up server on the way; with your own key it goes straight from your browser to the provider. The original photo is never stored. Only a small thumbnail is kept with the garment.

Logs

Each server-side photo reading is logged (time, which model answered, how long it took, and the reading itself, never the photo) so the app can be improved and kept within its limits. Logs are deleted after 10 days.

Your own API keys

If you use your own Gemini or Groq key, it is stored only in your browser and sent only to that provider.

Sharing

Layer Up does not sell or share your data. It is a personal project, not a commercial service.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset all to clear this browser. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email varunkulkarni0995@gmail.com.
